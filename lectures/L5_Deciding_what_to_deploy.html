L5 · Deciding what to deploy · 24 Nov

Predict the wait before you measure it

Offline model

Everything runs at release:

wait ≈ RTF × utterance duration (+ load time on first use).

Re-decoding model

Last re-decode of the full buffer at release:

wait ≈ RTF × duration, and compute grows quadratically while speaking.

Streaming model

Only the last chunk and a flush remain:

wait ≈ constant, independent of duration.

Write your prediction in DECISIONS.md before the afternoon demo. The demo then tests it.

Making offline models stream: re-decode and agree

Re-decode

Every step seconds, transcribe the buffer again.

LocalAgreement-n

Commit the words on which the last n hypotheses agree; never retract a committed word (Macháček et al., 2023).

Falling behind

If one update takes longer than step, lag grows without bound.
