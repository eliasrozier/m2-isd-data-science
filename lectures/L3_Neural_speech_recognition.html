wav2vec 2.0: learn speech representations without transcripts

Feature encoder

A CNN on the raw waveform produces one vector about every 20 ms.

Context network

A transformer reads the whole utterance; some frames are masked.

Pre-training

Identify the true quantised frame among distractors (contrastive), on untranscribed audio.

Fine-tuning

Add a linear layer and train with CTC on transcribed speech.

Sizes used here: base ≈ 95 M parameters, large ≈ 317 M. The base-100h checkpoint saw only 100 h of labels: a weaker acoustic model shows LM gains more clearly.

CTC: sum over all alignments, then collapse

P(y | x) = Σ over alignments a that collapse to y of Πₜ P(aₜ | x)

h h ∅ e l l ∅ l o → merge repeats → h ∅ e l ∅ l o → drop ∅ → hello

Why the blank ∅

Separates true double letters (l ∅ l) and absorbs silence.

Greedy decoding

argmax per frame, then collapse: your TODO greedy_ctc.

The catch

Frame outputs are conditionally independent: no spelling or word knowledge between them.

L3 · Neural speech recognition · 17 Nov

Whisper: an encoder–decoder trained on weakly labelled web audio

Input

Log-mel spectrogram of a 30 s window (shorter audio is padded).

Decoder

Predicts text tokens one by one, with task tokens (language, transcribe, timestamps).

Implicit LM

The decoder is itself a language model conditioned on audio: no external LM needed.

Sizetinybasesmallmediumlarge
Parameters39 M74 M244 M769 M1 550 M

Known failure modes: hallucinated text on silence or noise, repeated phrases on long audio, formatting ("20" vs "twenty").

L3 · Neural speech recognition · 17 Nov

Transducers: built for streaming

Encoder

Audio → frames, processed in chunks with limited look-ahead.

Prediction network

Embeds the labels emitted so far: a small internal LM.

Joiner

Combines both to emit a label or a blank at each frame.

The demo reference is one: a 20 M-parameter zipformer transducer in ONNX. Measured here on one CPU core: real-time factor 0.02 (int8) and 0.04 (fp32) on a file.

Word error rate, and why normalisation matters

WER = (S + D + I) / N

Alignment

Minimum edit distance between reference and hypothesis words.

Can exceed 100 %

Insertions are unbounded: a hallucinating model can produce more errors than words.

Normalise first

Case, punctuation, numbers, spelling variants. "Mr. Smith paid $20" vs "mister smith paid twenty dollars".

Report the normaliser you used. Different normalisers make models incomparable.

Count model load time separately.
