MFCC: a compact, roughly decorrelated description of each 10 ms

Waveform

8 kHz (FSDD) or 16 kHz

Pre-emphasis

y[n] − 0.97·y[n−1]

Frames

25 ms window, 10 ms hop, Hamming

Power spectrum

|FFT|² per frame

Mel filterbank

40 triangular bands

Log

compresses dynamics

DCT

keep 13 coefficients

+ Δ, ΔΔ

39 dimensions per 10 ms

100 vectors per second of speech. The DCT decorrelates the log-mel energies, which justifies diagonal covariances in the GMMs.

Each HMM state emits MFCC vectors from a Gaussian mixture

bⱼ(o) = Σₘ cⱼₘ · 𝒩(o ; μⱼₘ, diag σ²ⱼₘ)

Mixtures

Several speakers and contexts per state: one Gaussian is too narrow.

Diagonal

Cheap and stable, thanks to the DCT decorrelation.

Parameters

per state: M·(2D + 1); D = 39. A model = S states × that, plus S² transitions.

One left-to-right HMM per word

λ = (π, A, B)

π: start in state 1; A: stay or move right; B: the GMMs.

Topology is a prior

Speech moves forward in time: no backward arcs. EM keeps zeros at zero.

Durations

A self-loop gives a geometric duration: mean 1/(1 − aⱼⱼ) frames.

A language model is a probability over word sequences

P(W) = Πᵢ P(wᵢ | w₁ … w_i−1) ≈ Πᵢ P(wᵢ | w_i−N+1 … w_i−1)

N-gram = Markov chain of order N − 1

The history is truncated to the last N − 1 words.

Maximum likelihood

P(c | a b) = count(a b c) / count(a b)

Pad with N − 1 <s> and one </s>.

The catch

Unseen n-grams get probability 0: one unseen event makes the whole sentence impossible.

Smoothing moves probability mass to unseen events

Add-k

P(c | h) = (count(h c) + k) / (count(h) + k·|V|)

Simple; too much mass on unseen events when |V| is large.

Interpolation (Jelinek–Mercer)

P(c | a b) = λ₃ P₃ + λ₂ P(c | b) + λ₁ P(c)

Backs off to shorter histories; λ tuned on held-out text.

Production systems use Kneser–Ney (absolute discounting + continuation counts); KenLM builds those on day 2.
